# TaskMind

## Project Vision

TaskMind is an AI-powered study management platform that helps students focus on studying instead of planning. It automatically creates study plans, manages daily tasks, reschedules incomplete work, tracks progress, and provides AI assistance—all in one place.

## Problem Statement

Students spend a significant amount of time planning their studies instead of actually studying. Existing tools are either simple to-do lists or rigid planners that cannot adapt when a student misses a task. As a result, students lose consistency, struggle to prioritize topics, and use multiple applications to manage their study routine.

## Solution

TaskMind provides an intelligent study management system that automatically plans daily study sessions, reschedules unfinished tasks, tracks progress, analyzes study patterns, and offers AI-powered assistance for planning and learning. It brings the entire study workflow into a single platform.

## Target Users

* School students
* College students
* Competitive exam aspirants
* Self-learners

## Primary Goal

The primary goal of TaskMind is to help students spend less time planning and more time studying by providing an intelligent, adaptive, and organized study management system.
